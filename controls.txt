===============================================================================
  THE MASTER  //  QUICK GUIDE
  Castle Danger Music Company
===============================================================================

The Master listens to your finished mix, compares it with how records in your
genre usually measure, and tells you what to fix, in plain English. Where one
of our plugins does the job (Dominatrix, Chop Shop, Flatliner, BuzzKill) it
tells you which one and how to set it.

It has two pages:
  ANALYZE   the report. Your audio passes through untouched.
            NEXT: PRESCRIBE takes you to the fix.
  FIX IT WITH MY PLUGINS
            The Master prescribes your Castle Danger plugins (which ones, in
            what order, on the bus and on your tracks), then sets every knob
            for you and tells you why. Five steps, shown on the left:
            LISTEN, PRESCRIBE, PLACE THE PLUGINS, APPLY, REFINE.
  OR USE BUILT-IN FIXES (top right of FIX IT)
            No Castle Danger plugins? The Master fixes the mix itself with
            its own tone EQ, dynamics, stereo, hum and loudness tools (AUTO
            FIX). BACK TO MY PLUGINS returns.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO USE IT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Put The Master LAST on your master channel, after your limiter or
     Dominatrix, so it hears exactly what you'll export.
  2. Press RESET, then play the whole song (or at least 30 seconds of the
     loudest chorus). An offline bounce/export works too: every sample is
     analysed even when the bounce runs faster than real time.
  3. Read THE VERDICT. Fix the red items first, then the yellow ones,
     in your mix, or let FIX IT do it (see below).
  4. Change something, press RESET and play it again.
  REPORT ON switches the report between YOUR MIX and AFTER FIXES.

The report is saved with your project. When you reopen the project you'll see
the last report until you play the song again.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ANALYSIS (top right)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  LISTEN    On: everything that plays is analysed. Off: the report freezes.
            You can automate LISTEN to analyse only one part of the song.
  RESET     Forget what's been heard and start a new analysis.
  PROFILES  Import an updated genre profile pack, open the profiles folder,
            or go back to the factory profiles.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REFERENCE GENRE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  AUTO      The Master works out which genre your mix sounds closest to and
            compares against that. The yellow lamp marks the match.
  ROCK / METAL / PUNK / HARD ROCK / POP
            Lock the comparison to one genre (it's saved with the project).

  SOUNDS LIKE shows the closest genre and how sure the guess is (the % is
  split across all the genres, so it is NOT a score), plus the runner-up.
  How well the mix fits the genre is the MIX SCORE (out of 100).
  Rock, hard rock and punk are close cousins, so a split like 45% / 35% is
  normal. If AUTO picks the wrong one, just choose your genre.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TONAL BALANCE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Ten bands from SUB (20-60 Hz) to AIR (12-20 kHz). The middle line is the
  average record in the genre; the dark band is where most records sit and
  the faint band is the outer edge. Your mix is the line of dots:
    cream    in range
    yellow   worth checking
    red      clearly off
  The number is how many dB your mix has more (+) or less (-) of that band
  than the typical record. Bands move together: if one band is way too loud,
  the others look a little low. Fix the biggest one first.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VITALS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  LOUDNESS        Integrated loudness of the whole song (LUFS).
  TRUE PEAK       Highest peak, including peaks between samples. Streaming
                  services ask for -1 dBTP or lower (red line).
  PUNCH (PLR)     Peak-to-loudness ratio: how far peaks stick out above the
                  average level. Low = squashed, high = spiky.
  LOUDNESS RANGE  How much the level changes between sections (LU).
  STEREO WIDTH    How loud the sides are compared with the centre.
  MONO LOW END    Below 120 Hz, how alike left and right are (1.00 = mono,
                  which is what you want for kick and bass).


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE VERDICT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The MIX SCORE (0-100) is how closely your mix matches the genre plus the
  technical basics (peaks, clipping, phase, hum). It's a guide, not a
  grade: a deliberate choice can be "off" and still be right for the song.

    red     FIX: a real problem (clipping, out-of-phase, way off the genre)
    yellow  CHECK: noticeably different from the genre; listen and decide
    green   GOOD: in range

  COPY puts the whole report on your clipboard.

  What The Master looks for:
    loudness for the genre, true peaks, digital clipping, over-compression
    or spiky peaks, big level swings, stereo width and phase, stereo bass,
    each of the ten tonal bands, and 50/60 Hz hum in the quiet parts.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BUILT-IN FIXES  (The Master corrects the mix itself, with override)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  On FIX IT, press OR USE BUILT-IN FIXES.
  1. Play the whole song (ANALYZE page) so The Master has heard it.
  2. Press AUTO FIX. Every section below gets set for you,
     aiming at the genre. The report switches to AFTER FIXES.
  3. Play the song again. THE CURE shows YOUR MIX score > AFTER FIXES score.
  4. Press AUTO FIX once more after that play-through: it measures what came
     out and fine-tunes the loudness to land on target.
  5. Override anything you like (see below). Then bounce.

  FIXES ON     all fixes on/off. Flip it to compare with your original.
  MATCH LEVELS plays the fixed version back at your mix's loudness, so you
               judge tone and punch, not just "louder". TURN IT OFF BEFORE YOU
               BOUNCE, or you'll lose the loudness.
  AMOUNT       how much of the EQ, leveler, peak tamer and width moves to
               use. 100 % = as set.

  OVERRIDING
    Every knob and fader works like normal. Yellow arrows/markers show what
    AUTO FIX chose. When you move something, that section's AUTO button
    turns yellow; click it to put the section back. Each section's ON switch
    turns it off entirely. Pressing AUTO FIX again resets everything.
    All settings save with your project and can be automated.

  TONE MATCH EQ  ten faders, the same bands as the analysis. AUTO FIX pulls
                 each band toward the genre (up to 6 dB, leaving a little of
                 your mix's character) without changing the overall level.
  DYNAMICS       LEVELER: slowly lifts quiet sections, turns sudden loud
                 entrances down (for big level swings).
                 TAME PEAKS: look-ahead limiting of the peaks that stick out
                 of the mix (usually kick and snare); the threshold follows
                 the music.
  STEREO         WIDTH above 120 Hz (100 % = unchanged); BASS MONO makes
                 everything below 120 Hz mono.
  HUM            notches 50 or 60 Hz mains hum and 2 harmonics. BuzzKill on
                 the guitar/bass tracks is the better fix: it doesn't touch
                 the bass notes.
  LOUDNESS       GAIN pushes the mix into a true-peak limiter at the
                 CEILING: -1.0 dBTP for streaming, -2.0 extra safe for
                 MP3/AAC, -0.3 for CD/download masters.

  What AUTO FIX can't do (listed under FIX THESE IN THE MIX):
    clipping that's already in the mix, a mix that's already over-squashed,
    and out-of-phase tracks. Those have to be fixed at the source.

  Latency: with or without fixes, The Master delays the audio by about
  8 ms (reported to your DAW, which compensates automatically).


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FIX IT WITH MY PLUGINS  (the main way: The Master sets your plugins for you)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  This is what FIX IT opens on. Instead of fixing the mix itself, The
  Master tells you which Castle Danger plugins to put on
  the master bus, then sets their controls for you over Castle Danger Link
  (automatic, offline, on your computer only).

  Needs Dominatrix 1.2+, Chop Shop 1.1+ and, for tracks, The Servant 0.4+,
  BuzzKill 2.1+ and Flatliner 1.1+.

  1. Play the whole song with only The Master on the master bus.
  2. PRESCRIBE. THE PRESCRIPTION lists the chain, in order, for example:
        1 CHOP SHOP   shaves the peaks that stick out
        2 DOMINATRIX  glue, colour, stereo, loudness into the -1 dB ceiling
        3 THE MASTER  last: measures, plus the tone EQ and a safety limiter
     plus advice for individual tracks (BuzzKill for hum, Flatliner...).
  3. Put the plugins on the master bus in that order, BEFORE The Master.
     Each one shows LINKED as soon as it's found. Play a few seconds and The
     Master checks they're in the right order (it compares their levels).
  4. APPLY. Nothing changes until you press it. The Master sets every
     control on the plugins, exactly as if you'd turned the knobs (your DAW
     sees it: undo and automation work). WHAT I CHANGED & WHY lists every
     setting, old > new, with the reason. The lamp turns green when the
     plugin confirms.
  5. Play the song through, then REFINE: The Master measures what actually
     came out and fine-tunes (mostly Dominatrix's OUTPUT for loudness, Chop
     Shop's CEILING if the peaks still stick out), and explains each step.
  Override anything on the plugins themselves at any time.
  NEW PRESCRIPTION starts over (take the plugins off or bypass them and
  play the song again first, so The Master hears the untreated mix).

  THE SERVANT ON YOUR TRACKS (The Servant 0.4 or later)
  Put The Servant on your tracks and set each one's ROLE (Lead, Rhythm,
  Bass, Kick, Snare, Drums, Keys / Pads, Other). Each Servant tells The
  Master what its track sends to the mix, band by band. The Master works out
  each track's real level in the mix (faders, buses and sends included) by
  comparing their level fingerprints with what it hears, and then:
    TONE   shares the mix's tone correction out to the tracks that make
           each band: the track that makes most of the mud gets most of the
           cut. Kick and bass keep the low end, the lead keeps its presence.
           Up to 6 moves per track (The Servant's 6 tone bands).
    LEVEL  TRIMs the lead up if it's buried (or down if it's way on top).
    COMP   compresses the tracks that make the mix's peaks, and a lead that
           jumps around.
  All of it comes from the genre's MIX profile: no instrument profiles and
  no stem splitting. The tracks appear under YOUR TRACKS with their share
  of the mix; APPLY sets them along with the master bus, and every change
  is listed with its reason. Whatever the tracks can't fix, The Master's own
  TONE MATCH EQ does, followed by a -1 dBTP safety limiter.
  The Servants are run from here: RESET on The Master starts every Servant
  listening, and PRESCRIBE first has each one finish and decide its own
  CLEAN-UP and RESONANCES from its track and ROLE (it takes a moment: the
  button waits for them), then The Master plans the tone, level and comp.
  So: press RESET, play the whole song, then PRESCRIBE.

  BUZZKILL AND FLATLINER ON YOUR TRACKS
  They link by themselves too, and The Master works out which track each
  one is on (by track name, or by matching it to that track's Servant).
  They show under YOUR TRACKS with their name in yellow.
    BUZZKILL   RELEASE for the genre and the part (tight for metal and punk
               rhythm, natural for pop, gentler on leads and bass, a bit
               tighter on a noisy track); HEAR REMOVED off. The mix's hum
               and the track's noise floor are given as the reasons.
    FLATLINER  TARGET at the lead's average level (so the ride doesn't
               change the balance), RANGE from how much the lead swings and
               the genre, SPEED and GATE. A lead Flatliner rides doesn't
               also get The Servant's compressor for its jumps.
  If they're not there, the prescription asks for them when the mix needs
  them: BuzzKill when there's hum, Flatliner when the lead swings more than
  about 10 dB.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MY PROFILES  (your own, from songs you pick)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Aim at a sound of your own instead of a genre: a band, an album, your own
  best mix. REFERENCE GENRE > MY PROFILES > New profile from songs...
  (also in the PROFILES menu). Name it, and The Master makes a folder with
  that name and opens it. Drop in finished, mixed songs (WAV, AIFF, FLAC,
  MP3, OGG; M4A too on a Mac). One song is enough; three or more is better.
  The Master measures each song once, in the background, with the same
  analyser it uses on your mix, and keeps only the numbers (a hidden cache
  file in the folder). Add or remove songs any time; the profile follows.
  With one or two songs, how far your mix may stray is borrowed from the
  closest genre; with three or more it comes from how much your songs vary.

  Where the folders are:
    macOS:   ~/Library/Application Support/Castle Danger/The Master/
             Profiles/My Profiles/<name>/
    Windows: %APPDATA%\Castle Danger\The Master\Profiles\My Profiles\<name>\

  Pick one from MY PROFILES. It works everywhere a genre does: the report,
  the prescription for your plugins, the built-in fixes. AUTO also
  considers your profiles. The profile is saved inside your project, so it
  opens right on another computer too.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GENRE PROFILES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The genres are profiles: what records in each genre measure, built from
  reference songs. Updated profile packs may be posted on
  castledangermusic.com. To install one: PROFILES > Import profile pack...
  and pick the .json file. Every copy of The Master on your computer uses
  it. PROFILES > Restore factory profiles undoes it.
