===============================================================================
  THE MASTER  //  QUICK GUIDE
  Castle Danger Music Company
===============================================================================

The Master listens to your finished mix, compares it with how records in your
genre usually measure, and tells you what to fix, in plain English. Where one
of our plugins does the job (Dominatrix, Chop Shop, Flatliner, BuzzKill) it
tells you which one and how to set it.

It has two pages:
  ANALYZE   the report. Your audio passes through untouched.
  FIX IT    press AUTO FIX and The Master corrects what it found: tone, peaks,
            level swings, stereo, hum and loudness. Every correction is a
            normal control you can override.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO USE IT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Put The Master LAST on your master channel, after your limiter or
     Dominatrix, so it hears exactly what you'll export.
  2. Press RESET, then play the whole song (or at least 30 seconds of the
     loudest chorus). An offline bounce/export works too: every sample is
     analysed even when the bounce runs faster than real time.
  3. Read THE VERDICT. Fix the red items first, then the yellow ones,
     in your mix, or let FIX IT do it (see below).
  4. Change something, press RESET and play it again.
  REPORT ON switches the report between YOUR MIX and AFTER FIXES.

The report is saved with your project. When you reopen the project you'll see
the last report until you play the song again.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ANALYSIS (top right)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  LISTEN    On: everything that plays is analysed. Off: the report freezes.
            You can automate LISTEN to analyse only one part of the song.
  RESET     Forget what's been heard and start a new analysis.
  PROFILES  Import an updated genre profile pack, open the profiles folder,
            or go back to the factory profiles.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REFERENCE GENRE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  AUTO      The Master works out which genre your mix sounds closest to and
            compares against that. The yellow lamp marks the match.
  ROCK / METAL / PUNK / HARD ROCK / POP
            Lock the comparison to one genre (it's saved with the project).

  SOUNDS LIKE shows the closest genre and how sure it is, plus the runner-up.
  Rock, hard rock and punk are close cousins, so a split like 45% / 35% is
  normal. If AUTO picks the wrong one, just choose your genre.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TONAL BALANCE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Ten bands from SUB (20-60 Hz) to AIR (12-20 kHz). The middle line is the
  average record in the genre; the dark band is where most records sit and
  the faint band is the outer edge. Your mix is the line of dots:
    cream    in range
    yellow   worth checking
    red      clearly off
  The number is how many dB your mix has more (+) or less (-) of that band
  than the typical record. Bands move together: if one band is way too loud,
  the others look a little low. Fix the biggest one first.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VITALS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  LOUDNESS        Integrated loudness of the whole song (LUFS).
  TRUE PEAK       Highest peak, including peaks between samples. Streaming
                  services ask for -1 dBTP or lower (red line).
  PUNCH (PLR)     Peak-to-loudness ratio: how far peaks stick out above the
                  average level. Low = squashed, high = spiky.
  LOUDNESS RANGE  How much the level changes between sections (LU).
  STEREO WIDTH    How loud the sides are compared with the centre.
  MONO LOW END    Below 120 Hz, how alike left and right are (1.00 = mono,
                  which is what you want for kick and bass).


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE VERDICT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The MIX SCORE (0-100) is how closely your mix matches the genre plus the
  technical basics (peaks, clipping, phase, hum). It's a guide, not a
  grade: a deliberate choice can be "off" and still be right for the song.

    red     FIX: a real problem (clipping, out-of-phase, way off the genre)
    yellow  CHECK: noticeably different from the genre; listen and decide
    green   GOOD: in range

  COPY puts the whole report on your clipboard.

  What The Master looks for:
    loudness for the genre, true peaks, digital clipping, over-compression
    or spiky peaks, big level swings, stereo width and phase, stereo bass,
    each of the ten tonal bands, and 50/60 Hz hum in the quiet parts.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FIX IT  (automatic corrections, with override)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Play the whole song (ANALYZE page) so The Master has heard it.
  2. Go to FIX IT and press AUTO FIX. Every section below gets set for you,
     aiming at the genre. The report switches to AFTER FIXES.
  3. Play the song again. THE CURE shows YOUR MIX score > AFTER FIXES score.
  4. Press AUTO FIX once more after that play-through: it measures what came
     out and fine-tunes the loudness to land on target.
  5. Override anything you like (see below). Then bounce.

  FIXES ON     all fixes on/off. Flip it to compare with your original.
  MATCH LEVELS plays the fixed version back at your mix's loudness, so you
               judge tone and punch, not just "louder". TURN IT OFF BEFORE YOU
               BOUNCE, or you'll lose the loudness.
  AMOUNT       how much of the EQ, leveler, peak tamer and width moves to
               use. 100 % = as set.

  OVERRIDING
    Every knob and fader works like normal. Yellow arrows/markers show what
    AUTO FIX chose. When you move something, that section's AUTO button
    turns yellow; click it to put the section back. Each section's ON switch
    turns it off entirely. Pressing AUTO FIX again resets everything.
    All settings save with your project and can be automated.

  TONE MATCH EQ  ten faders, the same bands as the analysis. AUTO FIX pulls
                 each band toward the genre (up to 6 dB, leaving a little of
                 your mix's character) without changing the overall level.
  DYNAMICS       LEVELER: slowly lifts quiet sections, turns sudden loud
                 entrances down (for big level swings).
                 TAME PEAKS: look-ahead limiting of the peaks that stick out
                 of the mix (usually kick and snare); the threshold follows
                 the music.
  STEREO         WIDTH above 120 Hz (100 % = unchanged); BASS MONO makes
                 everything below 120 Hz mono.
  HUM            notches 50 or 60 Hz mains hum and 2 harmonics. BuzzKill on
                 the guitar/bass tracks is the better fix: it doesn't touch
                 the bass notes.
  LOUDNESS       GAIN pushes the mix into a true-peak limiter at the
                 CEILING: -1.0 dBTP for streaming, -2.0 extra safe for
                 MP3/AAC, -0.3 for CD/download masters.

  What AUTO FIX can't do (listed under FIX THESE IN THE MIX):
    clipping that's already in the mix, a mix that's already over-squashed,
    and out-of-phase tracks. Those have to be fixed at the source.

  Latency: with or without fixes, The Master delays the audio by about
  8 ms (reported to your DAW, which compensates automatically).


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GENRE PROFILES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The genres are profiles: what records in each genre measure, built from
  reference songs. Updated profile packs may be posted on
  castledangermusic.com. To install one: PROFILES > Import profile pack...
  and pick the .json file. Every copy of The Master on your computer uses
  it. PROFILES > Restore factory profiles undoes it.
