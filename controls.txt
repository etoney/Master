===============================================================================
  THE MASTER  //  QUICK GUIDE
  Castle Danger Music Company
===============================================================================

The Master listens to your mix while you're still mixing (a rough mix with the
levels roughly balanced is all it needs), compares it with how records in your
genre usually measure, and tells you what to fix, in plain English. Where one
of our plugins does the job (The Freak, Dominatrix, Cutthroat, Flatliner,
Dead Air) it
tells you which one and how to set it.

THE STEP BAR (under the genre bar) is the way through, five steps:
  LISTEN > REPORT > PRESCRIPTION > APPLY > REFINE
  The step you're on glows; finished steps say OK. Click any step you've
  reached to look at it again: LISTEN and REPORT show the report (your
  audio passes through untouched), the other three show the fix.
  THE BIG BUTTON next to it always does the next thing:
    PLAY YOUR SONG  until The Master has heard 20 seconds (it tells you how)
    PRESCRIBE >     on the report: opens the prescription and prescribes straight away
    PRESCRIBE / APPLY / REFINE  on the fix page, and UPDATE when you've
                    added or removed plugins on your tracks
  The fix: The Master prescribes your Castle Danger plugins (which ones, in
  what order, on the bus and on your tracks), then sets every knob for you
  and tells you why. The left column spells out the step you're on.
  EXPORT DIAGNOSTICS (next to COPY, and on the change log)
            Saves one text file with the report, the prescription, the
            change log and every linked plugin's settings and measurements.
            Send it to Castle Danger Music Company to fine-tune the system.
  ONE BUTTON on FIX IT always does the next step: PRESCRIBE, then APPLY,
            then REFINE. It says UPDATE when you've added or removed plugins
            on your tracks since the prescription.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO USE IT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Put The Master LAST on your master channel, after your limiter or
     Dominatrix, so it hears exactly what you'll export.
  2. Press RESET, then play the whole song (or at least 30 seconds of the
     loudest chorus). An offline bounce/export works too: every sample is
     analysed even when the bounce runs faster than real time.
  3. Read THE VERDICT. Fix the red items first, then the yellow ones,
     in your mix, or let FIX IT do it (see below).
  4. Change something, press RESET and play it again.
  REPORT ON switches the report between YOUR MIX and AFTER FIXES.

The report is saved with your project. When you reopen the project you'll see
the last report until you play the song again.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ANALYSIS (top right)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  LISTEN    On: everything that plays is analysed. Off: the report freezes.
            You can automate LISTEN to analyse only one part of the song.
  RESET     Forget what's been heard and start a new analysis. Once there's a
            prescription it asks: START OVER (plugins back to neutral, back to
            LISTEN) or MEASURE AGAIN (settings stay; the next play-through is
            scored and REFINE works from it).
  PROFILES  Import an updated genre profile pack, open the profiles folder,
            or go back to the factory profiles.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REFERENCE GENRE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  AUTO      The Master works out which genre your mix sounds closest to and
            compares against that. The yellow lamp marks the match.
  ROCK / HARD ROCK / PUNK / METAL
            Lock the comparison to one genre (it's saved with the project).

  SOUNDS LIKE shows the closest genre and how sure the guess is (the % is
  split across all the genres, so it is NOT a score), plus the runner-up.
  How well the mix fits the genre is the MIX SCORE (out of 100).
  Rock, hard rock and punk are close cousins, so a split like 45% / 35% is
  normal. If AUTO picks the wrong one, just choose your genre.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TONAL BALANCE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Ten bands from SUB (20-60 Hz) to AIR (12-20 kHz). The middle line is the
  average record in the genre; the dark band is where most records sit and
  the faint band is the outer edge. Your mix is the line of dots:
    cream    in range
    yellow   worth checking
    red      clearly off
  The number is how many dB your mix has more (+) or less (-) of that band
  than the typical record. Bands move together: if one band is way too loud,
  the others look a little low. Fix the biggest one first.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VITALS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  LOUDNESS        Integrated loudness of the whole song (LUFS).
  TRUE PEAK       Highest peak, including peaks between samples. Streaming
                  services ask for -1 dBTP or lower (red line).
  PUNCH (PLR)     Peak-to-loudness ratio: how far peaks stick out above the
                  average level. Low = squashed, high = spiky.
  LOUDNESS RANGE  How much the level changes between sections (LU).
  STEREO WIDTH    How loud the sides are compared with the centre.
  MONO LOW END    Below 120 Hz, how alike left and right are (1.00 = mono,
                  which is what you want for kick and bass).


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE VERDICT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The MIX SCORE (0-100) is how closely your mix matches the genre plus the
  technical basics (peaks, clipping, phase, hum). It's a guide, not a
  grade: a deliberate choice can be "off" and still be right for the song.

    red     FIX: a real problem (clipping, out-of-phase, way off the genre)
    yellow  CHECK: noticeably different from the genre; listen and decide
    green   GOOD: in range

  COPY puts the whole report on your clipboard.

  What The Master looks for:
    loudness for the genre, true peaks, digital clipping, over-compression
    or spiky peaks, big level swings, stereo width and phase, stereo bass,
    each of the ten tonal bands, and 50/60 Hz hum in the quiet parts.


  Latency: with or without fixes, The Master delays the audio by about
  8 ms (reported to your DAW, which compensates automatically).


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE FIX  (The Master sets your plugins for you)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The Master doesn't fix the mix itself: it tells you which Castle Danger
  plugins to put on
  the master bus, then sets their controls for you over Castle Danger Link
  (automatic, offline, on your computer only).

  Needs Dominatrix 1.3+, Cutthroat 1.2+, The Freak 1.0+ and, for tracks,
  The Servant 0.7+,
  Dead Air 2.1+, Flatliner 1.1+ and Low Blow 1.0+.

  OUTPUT (top of THE CURE)
    -6 dB  (default) a pre-master for a separate mastering step: peaks are
           held just under -6 dBFS (Dominatrix CEILING -6 dB, safety
           limiter -6 dBTP) and nothing is pushed for loudness. The report
           doesn't mark the mix down for being quieter than a record.
    -1 dB  a finished master: as loud as your genre, peaks at -1 dBTP for
           Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube.
    Flip it any time. After PRESCRIBE or APPLY The Master re-plans the
    loudness part from the same analysis (no need to play the song again)
    and sends the new settings.

  1. Set up: The Servant on every track (ROLE set), The Master last on
     the master bus and nothing else there. RESET, then play the whole song.
     The report's first line counts the Servants listening.
  2. PRESCRIBE (the big button) prescribes straight away; if it hadn't heard
     enough yet, keep playing and press PRESCRIBE. THE PRESCRIPTION lists
     the chain, in order, for example:
        1 THE FREAK   dynamic EQ: cuts the ranges that jump out, only when
                      they do (only prescribed when your mix needs it)
        2 CUTTHROAT   shaves the peaks that stick out
        3 DOMINATRIX  glue, colour, stereo; loudness into the -1 dB ceiling,
                      or peaks held at -6 dB with OUTPUT at -6 dB
        4 THE MASTER  last: measures, plus the tone EQ and a safety limiter
     and YOUR TRACKS: each track with the plugins to put on it, in order.
     Green lamp = linked to The Master, red ring = add it. The last row is
     the reminder: The Servant on every track you want balanced.
  3. Put the plugins on the master bus in that order, BEFORE The Master.
     Each one shows LINKED as soon as it's found. Play a few seconds and The
     Master checks they're in the right order (it compares their levels).
  4. APPLY. Nothing changes until you press it. The Master sets every
     control on the plugins, exactly as if you'd turned the knobs (your DAW
     sees it: undo and automation work). WHAT I CHANGED & WHY lists every
     setting, old > new, with the reason. The lamp turns green when the
     plugin confirms.
  5. Play the song through, then REFINE: The Master measures what actually
     came out and fine-tunes (mostly Dominatrix's OUTPUT for loudness, Chop
     Shop's CEILING if the peaks still stick out), and explains each step.
  Override anything on the plugins themselves at any time.

  UPDATE: added Dead Air, Flatliner, Low Blow or The Servant to a track (or
  took one off) after prescribing? The new one turns green as soon as it
  links, and the main button says UPDATE. Press it: The Master plans the
  tracks again from the same analysis (no need to play the song again) and,
  if you'd already applied, sends the new settings straight away. A Servant
  added later needs to hear its track first: play the song, then UPDATE.

  THE FREAK (dynamic EQ)
  While it listens, The Master measures how far each range (sub, bass, low,
  low mid, mid, hi mid, presence, bite, sheen) jumps above its usual share
  of the mix in the loud moments. A range that jumps more than is normal for
  it gets one of The Freak's six bands, set to cut 2-6 dB only while it
  jumps, with the threshold above the range's ordinary ups and downs. Jumps
  that happen on every beat (drum hits) are treated as the music, so it
  takes a lot more before The Freak touches them. The static tone balance
  stays with The Master's TONE MATCH EQ, so the two never fight. Each Freak
  band shows The Master's reason under its knobs.

  START OVER (top right of the prescription) is for big changes to the
  mix itself. It puts every Mix System plugin back to neutral (pass-through)
  and clears the prescription; play the song from the top, then press
  PRESCRIBE, so The Master hears the untreated mix. (PRESCRIBE does this
  for you if the bus plugins are still set from an earlier prescription.)
  With GENRE on AUTO, the report keeps judging against the genre the
  prescription aimed at until you start over.

  THE SERVANT ON YOUR TRACKS (The Servant 0.7 or later)
  Put The Servant on your tracks and set each one's ROLE (Lead Vocal,
  Backing Vocals, Lead Guitar, Rhythm Guitar, Bass, Kick, Snare, Drums,
  Keys / Pads, Other). Lead Vocal and Lead Guitar are both "the lead": kept
  on top. Backing Vocals are kept about 6 dB under the lead. Each Servant tells The
  Master what its track sends to the mix, band by band. The Master works out
  each track's real level in the mix (faders, buses and sends included) by
  comparing their level fingerprints with what it hears, and then:
    TONE   shares the mix's tone correction out to the tracks that make
           each band: the track that makes most of the mud gets most of the
           cut. Kick and bass keep the low end, the lead keeps its presence.
           Up to 6 moves per track (The Servant's 6 tone bands).
    LEVEL  TRIMs the lead up if it's buried (or down if it's way on top).
    COMP   compresses the tracks that make the mix's peaks, and a lead that
           jumps around. The Servant's compressor STYLE follows the role
           (PUNCH for drums, SMOOTH for leads and vocals, TIGHT for bass, GLUE for
           the rest); The Master leaves a style you picked alone.
    TRANSIENTS sets The Servant's transient shaper: more ATTACK on kick and
           snare when the mix is flat or their hits are soft (more for
           metal; never on hits that are already hard), less
           on snare and kit when the mix's peaks stick out, a shorter kick
           SUSTAIN when the low end is crowded (or for metal), less kit ring
           when the low mids are thick, a softer pick attack on spiky
           rhythm guitars in a peaky mix.
    DE-ESS turns on The Servant's de-esser for tracks whose "s" sounds jump
           out (usually the lead vocal; never drums or bass), stronger when
           the mix's bite (4-8 kHz) is already hot.
  All of it comes from the genre's MIX profile: no instrument profiles and
  no stem splitting. YOUR TRACKS lists each track once, with the plugins
  to put on it in order (e.g. 1 DEAD AIR > 2 THE SERVANT). A green lamp
  means that plugin is linked to The Master; a red ring means add it
  (hover a track for the reason). The list scrolls when you have more
  tracks than fit. APPLY sets them along with the master bus, and every
  change is listed with its reason in WHAT I CHANGED & WHY. Whatever the tracks can't fix, The Master's own
  TONE MATCH EQ does, followed by a -1 dBTP safety limiter.
  The Servants are run from here: RESET on The Master starts every Servant
  listening, and PRESCRIBE first has each one finish and decide its own
  CLEAN-UP and RESONANCES from its track and ROLE (it takes a moment: the
  button waits for them), then The Master plans the tone, level and comp.
  So: press RESET, play the whole song, then PRESCRIBE.

  DEAD AIR, FLATLINER AND LOW BLOW ON YOUR TRACKS
  They link by themselves too, and The Master works out which track each
  one is on (by track name, or by matching it to that track's Servant).
  They show under YOUR TRACKS in the row of the track they're on, in the
  order they go: Dead Air (right after the amp sim), Flatliner, The
  Servant, Dominatrix, Cutthroat, then Low Blow.
    DEAD AIR   RELEASE for the genre and the part (tight for metal and punk
               rhythm, looser for rock, gentler on leads and bass, a bit
               tighter on a noisy track); HEAR REMOVED off. The mix's hum
               and the track's noise floor are given as the reasons.
    FLATLINER  TARGET at the lead's average level (so the ride doesn't
               change the balance), RANGE from how much the lead swings and
               the genre, SPEED and GATE. A lead Flatliner rides doesn't
               also get The Servant's compressor for its jumps.
    CUTTHROAT  on a kick, snare or drum track whose hits make the mix's
               peaks (Cutthroat 1.2+). CEILING a few dB under that track's
               loudest hit (deeper the more its hits stick out), MASTER mode,
               HARD knee (SOFT on a full kit), 2x. That track's Servant
               compressor is turned off: the clipper takes the peaks, with
               more punch. Asked for (red, ADD IT) when such a track has none.
    DOMINATRIX on a bass whose 250 Hz - 2 kHz is thin, so it disappears on
               phones and small speakers (Dominatrix 1.3+). Saturation only:
               TUBE for rock, WHIP for metal, punk and hard rock; DISCIPLINE
               0, CEILING OFF, MATCH on (the level doesn't change).
    A Cutthroat or Dominatrix counts as a track plugin when its DAW track
    name matches one of your Servants' tracks; the ones on the master bus
    stay the bus plugins.
    LOW BLOW   on the bass, with the kick on its sidechain. From the Kick
               and Bass Servants The Master finds where they fight and how
               evenly: DEPTH from the genre and the fight, CROSSOVER just
               above the kick's thump (LOWS ONLY, so the bass's growl stays),
               RELEASE from the gap between kick hits so the bass is back
               before the next one. It tells you if no kick is arriving on
               the sidechain.
  If they're not there, the prescription asks for them when the mix needs
  them: Dead Air when there's hum, Flatliner when the lead swings more than
  about 10 dB, Low Blow when kick and bass are within 10 dB of each other in
  the same low band.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MY PROFILES  (your own, from songs you pick)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Aim at a sound of your own instead of a genre: a band, an album, your own
  best mix. REFERENCE GENRE > MY PROFILES > New profile from songs...
  (also in the PROFILES menu). Name it, and The Master makes a folder with
  that name and opens it. Drop in finished, mixed songs (WAV, AIFF, FLAC,
  MP3, OGG; M4A too on a Mac). One song is enough; three or more is better.
  The Master measures each song once, in the background, with the same
  analyser it uses on your mix, and keeps only the numbers (a hidden cache
  file in the folder). Add or remove songs any time; the profile follows.
  With one or two songs, how far your mix may stray is borrowed from the
  closest genre; with three or more it comes from how much your songs vary.

  Where the folders are:
    macOS:   ~/Library/Application Support/Castle Danger/The Master/
             Profiles/My Profiles/<name>/
    Windows: %APPDATA%\Castle Danger\The Master\Profiles\My Profiles\<name>\

  Pick one from MY PROFILES. It works everywhere a genre does: the report,
  the prescription for your plugins. AUTO also
  considers your profiles. The profile is saved inside your project, so it
  opens right on another computer too.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GENRE PROFILES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The genres are profiles: what records in each genre measure, built from
  reference songs. Updated profile packs may be posted on
  castledangermusic.com. To install one: PROFILES > Import profile pack...
  and pick the .json file. Every copy of The Master on your computer uses
  it. PROFILES > Restore factory profiles undoes it.

REBEL (on The Freak, Cutthroat, Dominatrix, Dead Air, Flatliner and Low Blow; not The Servant)
  A plugin with REBEL on is left exactly as you set it: The Master sends it nothing, still hears what it
  does, and plans the rest around it. A Cutthroat or Dominatrix you put on a track without being asked
  gets REBEL switched on by The Master, once; turn it off to hand that plugin over.
